Boredom-Maxxing Design

Point of view: a quiet page with one warm, heavy thing on it. Every screen gets exactly one ember slab for the thing that matters right now: the next class, the streak, today's quote or the lock count. Everything else sits on calm stone surfaces. Orange is only for acting and for data. Ink (bone in dark mode) is only for navigation.

Each row shows the same screen in light and dark. iPhone frame: 390 × 844 points, cropped to full content length. Fonts are SF Pro and SF Pro Rounded (ui-rounded) only.

  • Ground #EEECE7 / #0E0D0C
  • Surface #FFF / #1B1A18
  • Slab (focal) #2E1A10 / #3B1C0D
  • Action + data #FF5B1F / #FF6A2E
  • Navigation #161513 / #EDEAE4

Today

The week strip replaces the arrows and the repeated weekday title. The next class becomes the slab. All classes sit in one grouped list, and a row opens in place to show its briefing; "Cancel class" lives inside the open row. Only the first unfinished task gets the orange button. The fixed-height scrim stops the tab bar from colliding with content.

Class detail #/class/<session_id>

The page the Calendar alert opens. The class itself is the slab, the briefing is open by default and is the only reading surface, and cancelling sits last as a quiet destructive action with its rule spelled out.

Progress

The heatmap becomes the slab. The tiles put the label under the number (no caps eyebrows). The 14 study-time rows become a single column chart with its total. The note (the screen's only action) moves above study time.

Mind

The quote is the slab. "Add a quote" collapses into one row under it. All 40 quotes share one list, and your own quotes are marked "Yours" with Remove beside them.

More

Five equal sections become one slab (the lock count) and two grouped lists: Setup, where each item opens in place, and Bridge, which shows the host name instead of a raw URL.

Today: offline and empty

The offline banner sticks under the status bar. A day with no classes shows a teaching empty state instead of the slab. Before 06:00, the three tasks show as one locked card. Every mutation is dimmed.

Component states

Task states that do not fit on one day: judging, passed on appeal, not generated yet. Also the replacement form open, the toast, loading skeletons and no study data.
